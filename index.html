SAM-SILVA FOOTBALL ACADEMY MAKES HISTORY AS FIVE GIRLS LEAVE NIGERIA FOR ABROAD

By News Desk | Apa, Benue State | 12th November, 2026

History was made today as Sam-Silva Football Academy, one of the fastest-rising grassroots football academies in Benue State, sent five of its female players out of the country to pursue their professional football careers.

The five girls, who were discovered and nurtured from the rural communities of Apa and Agatu Local Government Areas, have left Ugbokpo and are presently in Lagos, preparing to board their flight out of Nigeria.

THE PLAYERS

  1. William Igbe Destiny – from Ugbobi, Apa
  2. Gawon Grace Ocholeye – from Ijaha, Ikobi, Apa
  3. Omiya Rosemary Timothy – from Obagaji, Agatu
  4. Odaliko Peace Timothy – from Obagaji, Agatu
  5. Joy Sule – from Oshigbudu, Agatu

FROM GRASSROOTS TO THE WORLD

Sam-Silva Football Academy was founded with one vision: to give talented children from rural communities the opportunity to be seen by the world.

Many of these girls started training on bare village fields with no boots, no jerseys, and no sponsors. What they had was talent, discipline, and a coach who believed in them.

The Academy scouted them during local tournaments and community matches in Apa and Agatu. They were signed after showing exceptional skill, speed, and dedication. Since then, they have been in camp at Sule Hotel, Apa Local Government Area, where they have been put in good shape, both physically and mentally, before moving abroad.

Today, that dream has become a reality.

THE MEN BEHIND THE DREAM

The Academy was founded by Coach Alade Samuel Elaigwu, the Founder and CEO.

Coach Elaigwu is a grassroots football developer who has spent years sacrificing his time, resources, and energy to raise young talents from Apa. From organizing local competitions to personally feeding and housing players in camp, his history is one of selfless service to youth development. Many young players in Benue State today owe their breakthrough to his vision and patience.

The journey of these five girls would not have been possible without the financial backbone of the Academy, Onehi Williams Otache, the Secretary General.

Otache has consistently supported the Academy financially, from camping, feeding, transportation, to documentation for the players. His quiet but steady support has kept the Academy alive when things were difficult.

The Academy also expresses deep gratitude to its major sponsor, Dr. Jeff Isima, whose sponsorship made this international movement possible. Dr. Isima's intervention has changed the lives of these five girls and their families forever.

A NEW DAWN FOR APA AND AGATU

Speaking from Lagos, Coach Elaigwu said the Academy is just getting started.

“These girls are ambassadors of Apa, Agatu, Benue State, and Nigeria. We trained them to be disciplined on and off the pitch. The world will hear about Sam-Silva Football Academy,” he said.

Parents and community leaders in Apa and Agatu have described the development as a source of pride and a clear proof that talent from the village can shine on the international stage.

Sam-Silva Football Academy is now calling on government, well-meaning individuals, and corporate bodies to support its mission of discovering and developing more talents.

For many young girls in Apa and Agatu, today is proof that dreams are valid.
